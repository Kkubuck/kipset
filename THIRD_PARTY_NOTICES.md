## Workout and energy references

- Named benchmark and Hero workout prescriptions link to the relevant CrossFit.com or CrossFit Library reference inside the app.
- Activity intensity and running MET values reference the 2024 Adult Compendium of Physical Activities.
- The app links to the official PAR-Q+ publisher for readiness information; PAR-Q+ questionnaire text is not copied or modified inside KIPSET.

Facts, titles, and source links are used for identification and attribution. KIPSET's difficulty, muscle-emphasis, scaling, and calorie classifications are original editorial additions and are not official classifications from the referenced organizations.
